# BSc Thesis

**Abdurakhmon's BSc Thesis: Design and Implementation of a Hardware-in-the-Loop Traction Control System for Electric Vehicles**

Abdurakhmon, a BSc Electrical Engineering student at the University of Debrecen, is currently working on his thesis titled "Design and Implementation of a Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) Traction Control System for Electric Vehicles". The thesis focuses on developing a traction control system for electric vehicles using a Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) approach.

The project employs CODESYS, a popular development environment for industrial control systems, and Python, a versatile programming language. Specifically, Abdurakhmon is utilizing CODESYS to design and implement the control logic, while Python is used for modeling the vehicle dynamics using Pacejka tire models. The HIL approach enables the testing and validation of the control system on a simulated environment, reducing the need for physical prototypes.

As of now, Abdurakhmon has made significant progress on his thesis, with a functional workspace containing various files and folders related to the project. His workspace includes CODESYS files, Python scripts, and graphical results from simulations.

This thesis project aligns with Abdurakhmon's career goals in the field of electrical engineering and control systems. His experience with HIL systems, CODESYS, and Python will be valuable assets in his future endeavors. With a graduation date set for February 2027, Abdurakhmon's thesis project demonstrates his capabilities in designing and implementing complex control systems, preparing him for a successful career in the industry. The skills and knowledge gained from this project will also complement his related projects, such as the Real-Time HIL EV Traction Control System and HIL Drive-by-Wire Control System.
